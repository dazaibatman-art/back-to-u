Terms of Service & Community Disclaimer

Important Legal Disclaimer: Group No1 operates strictly as a coordination platform to facilitate discovery and private communication between finders and owners. Group No1 does not take possession of lost items, does not inspect physical goods, and cannot guarantee that every reported item or ownership claim is genuine.

1. Honest Reporting

Users must only submit reports for genuine items they have found. False reports, spam, unlawful property, or misleading descriptions will result in immediate account suspension.

2. Ownership Verification & Fraud Prevention

Claimants are required to provide verifiable ownership proof. Submitting fraudulent claims for property that does not belong to you is illegal and strictly prohibited.

3. Safe In-Person Handoff Guidelines

For your physical safety, we strongly recommend conducting physical returns in well-lit, public locations such as local police stations, campus security desks, or public transit offices during daylight hours. Never meet in secluded areas or share unnecessary financial credentials.
