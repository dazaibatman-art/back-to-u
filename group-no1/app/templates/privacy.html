Privacy Policy

1. Protection of Private Verification Details

When you report a found item, the private verification details (such as serial numbers, secret scratches, or internal wallet contents) are stored securely in our database and are never displayed in public searches, public item cards, or search engine indices. They are accessible exclusively to the reporting finder and authorized reviewers.

2. Contact Information Confidentiality

Your email address and phone number are never made public by default. Group No1 provides built-in safe in-app messaging so you can coordinate verification and returns without disclosing personal phone numbers or home addresses.

3. Password Security & Data Integrity

All user passwords are encrypted using state-of-the-art cryptographic hashing (scrypt) with salted keys before persistence. Plaintext passwords are never recorded in database tables, system caches, or server application logs.
